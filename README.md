# placement-and-career-preparation-

What is the problem you’re solving?
Students often feel confused about which role they have to apply.
Tracking companies and roles that are best suitable for their skills and resume.

Who are the target users?
Primary targeted users are students and then the placements departments of the colleges.

What are their pain points?
Repeated process of reading the JD manually and creating the resume which is confusing for preparation and resume structure.
Feeling confused and stressed about their preparation and readiness.
Too many deadlines for multiple companies and roles.
Burnout due to multiple resources and no proper path.

What do people do today, without this app?
People often apply without a proper plan and apply to the random jobs which may not be relevant to their skills.
Apply to the jobs which are not suitable for them and miss the opportunities which are relevant to them.

Whose time and effort are you saving by solving this problem,and how?
Students' time and efforts will be saved because they are finding the jobs which are relevant to their skills and resume. so, they can focus on growing their skills rather than worrying about getting a job.
